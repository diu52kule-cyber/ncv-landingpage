Refund Policy

We issue refunds when a service fault is confirmed on our side. Our goal is to fix issues quickly, but if we cannot, we make it right.

  • Eligible cases include service outages caused by us, duplicate billing, or failure to deliver agreed features.
  • Contact us within 14 days of the issue with your invoice or payment reference.
  • We review requests within 5 business days and confirm next steps.
  • Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7–14 business days.

Refunds do not apply to issues caused by customer network outages, misuse, or third-party providers outside our control.

Privacy Policy

We collect only the information needed to deliver and support our services.

  • Data we collect: contact details, demo requests, service usage analytics, and billing information.
  • How we use it: to provide services, improve performance, and respond to support requests.
  • Sharing: we do not sell personal data. We only share with trusted processors (payment or hosting providers) when required to deliver the service.
  • Security: we use standard safeguards to protect data, including access controls and encryption where appropriate.
  • Retention: data is kept only as long as necessary for operational, legal, or billing needs.

For privacy questions or data requests, email nocashvends@gmail.com.

Return Policy

We do not accept returns. Our products are software services and custom deployments, so returns are not applicable.

  • If a fault is confirmed on our side, we will repair the issue or provide a refund in line with the Refund Policy.
  • For any hardware or kiosk arrangements, service and replacement terms are handled through the signed agreement.

We always aim to resolve issues fairly and quickly.
