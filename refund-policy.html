Refund Policy

Last updated: 29 May 2026

We issue refunds when a fault is confirmed on our side. We aim to resolve issues quickly and fairly.

Policies overview Privacy Policy Return Policy

When refunds apply

  • Confirmed service outages or failures caused by NoCashVends.
  • Duplicate billing or incorrect charges on our side.
  • Failure to deliver agreed features within the confirmed scope.

How to request a refund

  • Contact us within 14 days of the issue at nocashvends@gmail.com.
  • Include your invoice number or payment reference and a short description.
  • We review requests within 5 business days and confirm the outcome.

Processing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7–14 business days. If we can fix the issue promptly, we may resolve it before issuing a refund.

Not covered

Refunds do not apply to issues caused by customer network outages, misuse, or third-party service disruptions outside our control.
